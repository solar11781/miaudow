# Software Requirements (SRS)

This document lists the software requirements of the current design only. It is PROVISIONAL v0.4 of 2026-11-13,
updated with the team's later decisions up to 2026-11-15.
`[DATA]` marks requirements that depend on data not yet audited.
The priority column uses M = must (MVP), S = should and C = could (stretch).
The evidence is listed in REFERENCES.md and DATA-SOURCES.md, and the screens and data contract are in UI-UX-SPEC.md.

## Functional requirements

| ID | Pri | Requirement | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | M | Province line: Nghệ An shown as the default and only value, locked (other provinces come through the country adapter, ARCHITECTURE.md §1b) | No other province is listed or selectable (UI-UX-SPEC §7) |
| FR-02 | M | User selects land type from picture cards using the official commune wording ("cao cưỡng", "cuối kênh", "thiếu nước") with "không chắc, hỏi cán bộ khuyến nông" | The answer selects regional content only, never a farm-specific number; no plan is filtered out by the land-type answer: every plan is shown; the land-type answer carries the water situation: there is no per-season water-reliability input and no plan is hidden by a water answer; water facts are shown for every plan |
| FR-04 | M | User picks past years (1995-2024) to compare | Label "past year, not a forecast" is always visible; each year row shows the year and, for each official hazard, the share of Nghệ An farmland where the QĐ 18/2021/QĐ-TTg risk-level rule was met (heavy rain Art. 44, heat Art. 47, drought rain part Art. 48, damaging cold Art. 53; ML-DESIGN.md §5a), computed per NASA POWER cell over the days and months in which any P-02 crop was in the field in that calendar year, level 1 or higher counted as "rule met", whole percent; no yes/no cut-off, no summary box, no terciles; the drought rain part (monthly rain more than 50% below the 1991-2020 mean, "kéo dài từ 2 tháng đến 3 tháng") is shown with the rule's wording and both counts (consecutive months; any 2 months); the limitation "we apply the official thresholds to NASA's 50 km grid values; gridded values smooth extremes (Zhang et al. 2011, abstract)", the limitation that issued levels weigh more factors than the tables, so ours is a mechanical reading (R-K03), the cells note (the values come from {n} NASA weather squares of about 50 km), the midnight-rain note (daily totals can miss heavy rain that crosses midnight) and the mixed-spell note (heat or cold spells whose days fall in different temperature bands are not counted) are shown (five lines, UI-UX-SPEC §8). The basic definitions (QĐ 18/2021/QĐ-TTg Art. 5, R-A01; TT 25/2022/TT-BTNMT Art. 3.6) are ML features only, never displayed labels, with strict thresholds as the articles word them: heat day Tmax strictly above 35 C (Art. 5.21 "vượt quá 35°c") over the whole season window (flowering dates are not sourced); heavy rain > 50 mm in 24 h (Art. 5.17); drought rain condition, both readings, against a rain normal from training years only; rét hại daily mean strictly below 13 C (Art. 5.18 "dưới 13oc"), rét đậm < 15 C (TT 25/2022 Art. 3.6). The displayed risk-level labels use Art. 47 and 53, with 35.0 C and 13.0 C included |
| FR-05 | M | User answers one tap-button question for the single most important priority (income, stability, water, land care) | The matching row of every plan card is highlighted; plans are never scored or reordered; no sliders or weights. Design informed by abstract-only, non-farmer studies; provisional until tested with users. Response time target: see NFR-03 |
| FR-06 | M | App shows the four P-02 structures CC1-CC4 and the farmers' usual structure CC5, plus the P-03 local control (rice - rice - fallow) as a second description of local practice | All shown, unranked; both local-practice descriptions labelled with their study (P-02 and P-03 disagree at the same site); each plan worded "each crop tested in its season on this land type (P-02, 2020-21)", never as a tested whole-year sequence |
| FR-07 | M | Month timeline (Jan-Dec) per candidate | Bars use the sowing and harvest dates reported for every crop in P-02 Tables 1, 5 and 9; where reported dates overlap (CC1 mung bean harvest 30/8-3/9 vs maize sown 1/9; CC5 black bean harvest 25/8 vs sweet potato sown 15/8) the overlap is drawn as reported with the note that the paper does not say whether one field carried the whole sequence |
| FR-08 | M | Trade-off cards per candidate: money (P-02 Table 13 net profit incl. labour per crop, with the total labelled as our arithmetic and the price year 2020-21), stability (official-condition shares per crop; real rice history, with the out-of-sample prediction only for 2016-2024), water (sourced facts per crop; a rice crop shows the TCVN 8641 rows of its own season), land care (three separate checks) | Each value has its real unit or "not available" / "not listed in the source"; rice history shown as lowest / middle / highest past value with the worst years named from 2 picked years, or that year's value when 1 is picked; the model's 80% range shown next to its out-of-sample prediction only when G4 passes for that season; none is shown without a trust label of source facts; contract: UI-UX-SPEC §11 |
| FR-09 | M | Plans shown as one card per plan with identical rows in real units | No score, rank, tie rule or dominance flag in the output; every card states the P-02 limitations: single unreplicated 1,000 m2 plots, one year; plot layout unknown; reported date overlaps; methods list vs results tables conflict on CC2-CC4 and two identical Table 4 rows |
| FR-10 | M | Tapping any value shows its source, the trust label of source facts only (study type, place, years, replicated or not, full text read) and the source's limitations (the `SourceFacts` fields of UI-UX-SPEC §10) | 100% of displayed values resolve to a source record (automated test) |
| FR-11 | M | Method page: data, target, validation, reference forecasts vs model table, per-season gates G1-G4 with results, coverage table, explain-the-terms section, limitations, PICSA sentence (R-U19) (UI-UX-SPEC §11b) | Numbers read from `artifacts/metrics.json` and `artifacts/gates.json` via `GET /api/method`, not hand-typed; the page states that the G2-G4 numbers (90% CI, three-province set, 70-90% coverage) are team choices; the claim "NASA weather improves the prediction" is made only if the model beats the same model without weather columns (BNW) on the test years |
| FR-12 | M | Vietnamese and English UI: starting language from the browser's language setting, toggle on every page | All UI strings come from `frontend/src/i18n/vi.ts` and `frontend/src/i18n/en.ts`; no missing keys (test) |
| FR-13 | S | User may enter expected price and cost per crop, per hectare; built only after the core works (UI-UX-SPEC §12d) | Output labeled as the study's yield with the user's prices and costs, not the field's harvest (revenue = study yield x price); net margin shown only when costs entered |
| FR-14 | S | Shareable one-page summary (print and share link; no picture export) | Includes sources and the picked past years |
| FR-15 | C | LLM "explain simply" button (first a later idea; built during the event and switched on for the hosted Hanoi version) | Off unless its server switch is set; app fully usable without it; the server builds the text from its own compare answer (the browser sends only a plan code and a field); uses member A's Claude API key, which lives only in the server environment (never in the frontend or the repository); generated text is marked as SI-generated (R-H15) |
| FR-16 | C | SMAP soil-moisture check; after the 11:00 Sunday submission (global round), earlier only if its research, testing and validation fit | Labeled observation date and resolution (36 km / 9 km) |
| FR-17 | M | Per-strategy explanation: why shown (each crop tested in its season on this land type in P-02, or local practice), evidence, benefits, information gaps, risks needing local review | Every shown strategy has all five fields filled from rules/rotations.yaml (test) |
| FR-18 | M | Bare periods shaded on each timeline (official ask) | Derived from season blocks; no hand-entered values; no erosion claim: bare months are not presented as erosion (Slaets et al. 2016); the land-care check counts days with a living crop out of 365 (NRCS "Maximize Continuous Living Roots") |
| FR-20 | M | Soil wetness per plan crop and picked past year, next to its 1991-2020 average | NASA POWER GWETROOT, farmland-weighted over the crop's field days; labelled "NASA model value (MERRA-2), not a measurement"; no category word or threshold (UI-UX-SPEC T36) |
| FR-21 | S | Method page (bonus): NASA grid figure and the satellite rain check, GPM IMERG vs NASA POWER monthly rain for Nghệ An, 2001-2024 | Correlation and both means shown; no pass or fail; "neither is ground truth" and the shared rain-gauge input stated (DATA-DICTIONARY.md §5c) |
| FR-22 | M | Guidance without a pick: an open "check before you change" row with the plan's risks | No ranking (H3; UI-UX-SPEC T38) |
| FR-23 | S | Priority line on CC1-CC4 comparing with the usual P-02 plan on the same measure (bonus) | Never says better, best, should or recommended (UI-UX-SPEC T37) |

## Nonfunctional requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Hosted web app (FastAPI on Railway) that reads only precomputed artifacts; no live external data call (no NASA, NSO or model call at request time); the exceptions are the optional Google sign-in and the calls to the Claude API for the "explain simply" button (FR-15) and the copilot, which must be stated | Backend tests pass with outbound network calls blocked; every farmer route works without sign-in |
| NFR-02 | Reproducible pipeline: one command per stage, pinned dependencies, raw data checksums | Fresh clone reproduces `artifacts/` (except raw downloads, documented) |
| NFR-03 | Loads in < 5 s on a laptop; interactions < 1 s (engineering target set by the team, no source) | Manual check before demo |
| NFR-04 | Usable on a phone-width screen (360 px; engineering target, no source) | Visual check with a mobile viewport |
| NFR-05 | Accessibility: tap targets at least 44 x 44 px (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.5; Apple HIG 44 pt); body text about 17 px by default (Apple HIG 17 pt default text) and resizable to 200% (WCAG SC 1.4.4); contrast at least 4.5:1 (WCAG SC 1.4.3); icons plus words; no color-only meaning | Checklist in ARCHITECTURE.md §7 |
| NFR-06 | No personal data collected on farmer routes; step answers stay in the URL fragment and reach the server only in the POST body of the compare call; our server's request log is off. Only exception: the optional Google sign-in, built after the core, stores the Google account id and the saved answers, with any typed prices and costs (at most 20 saved sets per account), no name, email or picture if Google allows that scope [UNVERIFIED], with a privacy notice and a delete-everything button (UI-UX-SPEC §12c) | Without sign-in: no server-side storage of inputs, no cookies, no localStorage writes, answers never in a request URL (UI-UX-SPEC test T14) |
| NFR-07 | Every library, model and dataset disclosed with version and license | README "Attribution" section generated from `pyproject` and DATA-SOURCES |
| NFR-08 | No leakage: test year data never used in normal-yield, scaling or feature fitting; the rain normal used in ML features comes from training years only | Unit tests on the splitter, normal-yield fitter and feature normals |
| NFR-09 | Scientific honesty: no displayed number without a source and a trust label of source facts | FR-10 test |
